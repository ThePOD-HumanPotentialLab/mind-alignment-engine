Privacy Notice

What we keep, why we keep it, and your rights over it.

This notice explains how The POD Human Potential Lab, part of POD Consulting Limited, Abuja, Nigeria, handles your information when you use the Mind Alignment Index, the 7-Day Mind Reset and the Personal Optimization Drill (POD). We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

  • Your assessment: your answers to the Mind Alignment Index, your scores and your pattern.
  • Your contact details: your name, your email address and, if you choose to give it, your WhatsApp number.
  • Your 7-Day Mind Reset: your progress and the evening reflections you submit.
  • Your POD, if you join: your preparation answers (such as your top five goals, faith mode and chosen hour), your daily check-ins and Mirror Moments, any questions you ask the Optimizer, and your payment reference. Card details are handled by our payment provider, never by us.

Some things stay only on your own phone and never reach us: your half-written reflections, your morning intentions and your place in an unfinished assessment.

We never ask for, and never keep, what you write during the silent hour or to God.

Why we use it

  • To show you your result and run the programmes you start.
  • To contact you about your development and our programmes. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • To improve the Mind Alignment Index, using combined results with names and contact details removed.

We rely on your consent, which you give when you tick the box, and on what is needed to deliver the programme you asked for.

Who else handles it

We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers. A few trusted providers handle it on our behalf:

  • Cloudflare hosts this website and its database.
  • Paystack processes POD payments.
  • Anthropic powers the Optimizer, Josh Ochu's AI Mind Coach. When the Optimizer replies to you, the words you share with it and your POD preparation answers are sent to it to write that reply.

How long we keep it

For as long as you take part in our programmes, and no longer than three years after your last activity, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

You may ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done before. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
