Questions

Good to know

When does it start?

The series premieres on 27 December 2026, with a new story and family session every week for 52 weeks.

What ages is it for?

Children aged 3 to 11. Each session's questions are written so younger and older children can both join in.

I'm a single mum. Is this for me too?

Absolutely. "Papa" in the series stands for any present, loving adult: mums, uncles, grandparents and guardians all lead the session beautifully.

What's free and what's in the membership?

The animated stories are free on YouTube. The membership adds the full weekly family session: the devotional entry, the conversation questions, an audio version, a printable activity and the sign-off.

How do I pay?

Payments are processed securely by Selar in naira or US dollars. Monthly plans renew each month and can be cancelled anytime; yearly plans are a single payment.

What is "Our Year with Papa"?

Every session ends with a Memory Moment: a selfie together and a printable Memory Page with the week's verse, what you talked about and what your child said. Week by week, your family builds its own keepsake book. Yearly and Founding Family members also get a beautiful cover and title page to bind it all together. Sharing photos online is always optional; your memories stay yours.

Can our church or school use it?

Yes. We offer licences for children's ministries and Christian schools. Message us on WhatsApp or email and we'll tailor one to your size.
